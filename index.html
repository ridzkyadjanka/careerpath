Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. CareerPath uses a device-local workspace. Your progress is saved in this browser, and you can export a copy from My Profile.

Is the assessment a psychometric test?

No. It is a reflection tool. Scores show the share of your answers aligned with each career family, not your chances of getting a job.

Does the CV analyzer use AI?

Not yet. The current version uses transparent rules: it checks English keywords, common section headings, and numbers that show impact.

Can I upload a PDF or Word CV?

For now, paste your CV text. File upload is planned for a future version.
